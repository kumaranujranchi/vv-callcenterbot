## Technical Troubleshooting Guide for Agents

### Issue 1: Red Light on PON / LOS on Router
- **Cause**: Fiber optical cable disconnected, physically bent, or exchange level downtime.
- **Troubleshooting Steps for Customer**:
  1. Ask customer to check the thin yellow fiber cable behind the router. Ensure it is not tightly bent, crushed, or pulled out.
  2. Switch off router power plug, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on.
  3. If LOS continues flashing red for more than 5 minutes, optical power is lost from the distribution box (DP).
- **Agent Script to Customer**:
  *"Sir/Ma'am, LOS red light indicates a physical signal break. I have initiated an optical line test on your connection, and I am booking a priority technician visit within 4 hours. Your ticket number is TKT-[Generated]."*

### Issue 2: Slow Internet Speed / Wi-Fi Range Drop
- **Steps**:
  1. Ask customer if speed is slow on 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz band. Recommend connecting to "5G" SSID for speeds over 100 Mbps.
  2. Router should be placed at minimum 4 feet height in an open central room, away from microwaves and thick concrete walls.
  3. Advise speedtest only at `speedtest.net` with LAN cable or standing within 5 meters of router.

### Issue 3: Wi-Fi Password Change
- **Steps**:
  1. Open browser and type router IP: `192.168.1.1` (Default username: `admin`, password on sticker back of router).
  2. Go to **WLAN Settings** > **Security** > Enter new password (minimum 8 characters).
  3. Click **Apply / Save** and reconnect all phones.
