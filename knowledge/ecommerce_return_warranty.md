# E-Commerce & Retail Products Knowledge Base

## Return & Replacement Policy
- **Return Window**: 7 days from delivery date for Electronics & Gadgets, 14 days for Fashion & Apparel.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Item must be unused, unwashed, with original tags intact and in original brand box.
  - Serial number/IMEI must match our invoice records.
- **Non-returnable Items**: Innerwear, personal hygiene products, opened consumable items, software licenses.
- **Replacement Process**:
  - If defective or damaged upon arrival, replacement is dispatched within 24 hours of return pickup.
  - Customer must upload 2 clear photos of the damaged packaging and product via the order tracking link.

## Refund Timelines & Bank Crediting
- **UPI & Wallets**: 24 to 48 hours post-pickup verification.
- **Credit Card / Debit Card**: 5 to 7 business days depending on customer's issuing bank.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Customer must provide bank account IFSC and account number or UPI ID in the app. Refund processed within 3 business days.

## Warranty & Repairs
- All electronic products carry a minimum 1-year brand warranty.
- If product fails after 7 days, agent must direct customer to the nearest authorized brand service center with our invoice.
- Invoice can be re-sent to customer's registered WhatsApp and email in 1-click from agent CRM.
